Spaghetti Recipe

Description

This Mediterranean-style spaghetti is a hearty, delightful vegetarian dinner or a delicious complement to grilled chicken or fish. Flavored with olive oil, garlic, feta, and green olives, it’s light yet satisfying.

Ingredients

  • 1 (16 ounce) package spaghetti
  • 3/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • 6 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 1 cup roughly chopped pimento-stuffed olives
  • 1 cup crumbled feta cheese
  • 1/3 cup chopped sun-dried tomatoes
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh dill, divided
  • 1 teaspoon lemon zest
  • 3 tablespoons lemon juice
  • 2 teaspoons dried oregano
  • 2 teaspoons smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
  • lemon wedges
